## Information Visualization Project
- I worked on a school project that involved making visualizations in Tableau. While the data was provided I designed and picked all the visuals myself based of what I deemed as important or as a KPI.
  Then from my visuals I crafted both a dashboard and a story. I used a variety of chart types, calculated fields, table calculations, LOD calculations, and parameters.
